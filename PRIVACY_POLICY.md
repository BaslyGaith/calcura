# Privacy Policy

Calcura works entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- No analytics SDK.
- No advertising SDK.
- No `INTERNET` permission — the app cannot reach the network even if it wanted to.

All calculation history and settings are stored only on this device, using local storage
(a Room database for history, Jetpack DataStore for settings and memory). Nothing leaves
the device.

You can erase this data at any time from **Settings > Clear calculation history**, or by
clearing the app's storage from Android system settings.
